The forensic match report

From “what did I miss?” to “I’ll see that next time.”

A first-time player submits one finished game, finds the turning point, understands the consequence, and leaves with one question worth carrying into the next game.

Intent decision
Do not optimize for calmness. Optimize for credible clarity after uncertainty.

Journey intent

User goal

Understand the one moment that changed the game without reading an engine report.

Product promise

One turning point, one explanation, one practical question for the next game.

Trust proof

The board, notation, arrows, and explanation must describe the same position.

Critical states

Ready to submit

The user has enough context to choose a source without learning the product first.

Primary action
Explain my game
Reassurance
Public username only. No Chess.com password.
Success signal
Button changes to Opening your review…

Analysis in progress

Waiting is truthful and oriented around the user’s game, not a decorative spinner.

Show
Game name or source, current step, and what is being checked.
Preserve
Submitted source and route on refresh or interruption.
Recovery
Retry without re-entering the game.

Turning point found

The report opens on the most useful moment, not a dashboard overview.

First view
Before your move, with the relevant square and threat visible.
Next action
Show move, then Continue.
Completion
One habit for your next game.

Username not found

The problem is specific and recoverable. The user does not lose their input.

Message
We could not find a completed game for that username.
Action
Check spelling, try again, or paste a PGN instead.
Do not
Reset the form or say Something went wrong.

No meaningful turn

A steady game is a useful result, not a failure state.

Explain
Nothing swung far enough to single out one moment.
Action
Review another game or try a PGN.
Tone
Do not imply the player did something wrong.

Quota reached

The limit appears at the point of action with a clear alternative.

Message
You have used your two free reviews this week.
Action
See plans or sign in.
Trust
Never hide the limit until after analysis begins.
